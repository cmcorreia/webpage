large instrumentation consortia

2024–now

PCS — Planetary Camera and Spectrograph

E-ELT instrument. Involvement in the design and performance assessment of the Planetary Camera and Spectrograph for the E-ELT.

2020–2024

METIS

Mid-infrared ELT Imager and Spectrograph — an E-ELT first-light instrument. Contributions to adaptive-optics performance analysis and wavefront sensing.

W. M. Keck Observatory

Adaptive Optics Visiting Scientist. Projects: KAPA, RTC Upgrade, Predictive Control, PSF reconstruction.

2015–

HARMONI

The first-light spectroscopic capability for the European Extremely Large Telescope (E-ELT), providing visible and near-infrared integral field spectroscopy. Main tasks:

  • Real-time computer specification
  • Adaptive optics (SCAO and LTAO) performance assessment
  • Data post-processing

2010–2015

Raven (Subaru, w/ CNRC & UVic)

Developed at the University of Victoria Adaptive Optics Lab, Raven was the first Multi-Object Adaptive Optics (MOAO) technical and science demonstrator installed on an 8 m class telescope, the Subaru Telescope. Contributed to design specification, real-time tomographic wavefront reconstruction & control, and calibration — leading the advisory of several students and papers [see full list here].

2009–2015

TMT — NFIRAOS

TMT will be the first ground-based astronomy telescope designed with adaptive optics as an integral system element. The Narrow Field Infrared Adaptive Optics System (NFIRAOS) will be the first AO system deployed on TMT. Led efforts on the optimisation of sky-coverage through optimal dynamical estimation of tilt and tilt-anisoplanatism modes, and on PSF reconstruction from AO telemetry for this novel and demanding multi-conjugate mode.
