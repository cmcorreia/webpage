Carlos M. Correia

Associate Professor, University of Porto·Adaptive Optics & Astronomical Instrumentation

I work on wavefront sensing, atmospheric tomography and real-time control for adaptive-optics systems on ground-based telescopes — from Keck and Gemini to instrument concepts for the ELT. Based in Porto (FEUP / INESC TEC / CENTRA); previously LAM Marseille, NRC Herzberg, and W. M. Keck Observatory.
