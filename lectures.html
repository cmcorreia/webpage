    Recruitment

    Open positions in my group. I welcome motivated students and researchers working on topics aligned with my research in adaptive optics, wavefront sensing, and astronomical instrumentation.

    MSc Research Opportunities — Multiple Topics

    University of Porto – FEUP / INESC TEC / CENTRA

    I continuously supervise MSc dissertation projects on topics including:

    • Adaptive optics simulation and modelling
    • Wavefront sensing and reconstruction algorithms
    • PSF reconstruction from AO telemetry
    • Phase-retrieval techniques for space and ground-based optics
    • Real-time control and signal processing for AO systems
    • Optical design and performance analysis for ELT instruments (HARMONI, PCS, Ciao PCS, OPTIMA)

    A full list of specific dissertation topics will be posted here shortly. In the meantime, feel free to reach out: ccorreia_at_pm.me

     

    PhD and post-doctoral positions will be advertised here as they become available. Check back or get in touch to express interest.
